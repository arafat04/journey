Every time I want to do it, I want to do it perfectly, all or nothing, either it will be grand or there is no meaning of doing that. This mentality is prominent in 
every aspect of my life. When I start reading a book, I engage totally and only read that, last july, I was reading a book 10 days straight, did nothing in that period except cooking and reading. In terms of study, work everything. In my subconscious mind, I want stress, a short deadline so that I can have dopamine flooded in my blood, I feel alive, purposeful in life, can have the ultimate concentration, can forget all the traumas, guilt, fear everything and can enjoy life. I think that is why people procrastinate, they fear and when the deadline is short, we will definitely have cortisol and dopamine, they can forget everything and can live their life.

I do not have to do the things at once. Just doing little by litte can end up huge success in completing the tasks. This month I studied almost 90 hours (usually I study or work 140 hours per month - actual hours measured from pomodoro app, breaks are not counted) - but only spent 11 hours on research work and and in those hours I totally redesigned the research and did the whole system analysis. So even if I want to finish the whole work in 1 week( I can do it) - but it will enhance the synaptic connections and embolden the neural pathway for that belief.

Do small - regularly - consistently.
